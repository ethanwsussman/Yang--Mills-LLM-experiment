for every $`A,B\in L^2(\lambda)`$.

There is a unique translation-ergodic decomposition $`\lambda=\int\lambda_\xi\,\mathrm{d}\nu(\xi)`$, understood as the distribution of its conditional law on $`\mathcal X`$. On one common $`\nu`$-full set, every $`\lambda_\xi`$ is translation ergodic, invariant and reflection positive at every real translate of the planes in [(2)](ECM1.pdf), and
